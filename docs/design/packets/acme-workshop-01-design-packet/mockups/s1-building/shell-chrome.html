ACME Workshop — S1 mockup 1 of 3: the Building chrome

The Building (L1) is chrome and nothing else: a marking banner rendered from the runtime vocabulary, the application identity, the elevator (Floor navigation), the acting-as affordance, and an empty content slot where a Floor (L2) will mount from S2 onward. Two personas are drawn side by side because the difference between them is the deliverable: the same code, two manifests, two different Buildings. Everything here is fictional — names, manufacturers, markings and the marking vocabulary itself.

1 · Signed in — the same chrome, two subjects

2 · The states the chrome must have before a Floor exists

3 · What is deliberately not here

No Floor content, no Suite tabs, no Office toolbar, no device table, no map, no utility-window chrome, no group switcher, no theme picker. S1's product is the seam, not the surface: prove that the chrome around an empty room is built from identity and configuration, and every later slice fills a room without touching the Building.
