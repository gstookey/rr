ACME Workshop — S1 mockup 2 of 3: the Lobby at /

The Lobby is the Building's directory: it lists only the Floors this subject may enter, and that list is not a constant in the shell — it is /api/config's per-group manifest, rendered. Each variant below is printed beside the manifest fragment that produced it, so the seam is readable rather than asserted. At / the directory is the page; the elevator rail (see mockup 1) appears once you are standing on a Floor.

What is deliberately not here

No per-Floor status, counts, badges or activity — those need read models that do not exist until S2/S3, and a Lobby that summarises data it has not been authorised to read is exactly the leak this architecture is built to prevent. No group switcher (nobody in the seed holds two groups; register question Q2). No tenant theming beyond the token hooks in tokens.md — that is S4's proof, not S1's.
